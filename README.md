# Edge Finder

A single-page sports betting research tool. It runs entirely in your browser.

- **Games:** compares sportsbook prices against a de-vigged "fair" line (Pinnacle or market median) for moneylines, spreads, totals and player props, and flags value bets with a suggested stake.
- **Tailers:** logs picks from handicappers you follow and scores them on record, ROI and closing line value (CLV).
- **My bets:** tracks your own bets.
- **Paper account:** practice bankroll with an optional autopilot that places paper bets on value lines and grades them.

Odds come from [The Odds API](https://the-odds-api.com). Your API key and all your data are stored only in your browser (localStorage). Nothing is saved to this repository.

It never logs into a sportsbook or places real bets. For research and practice only.
